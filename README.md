A freight spend analysis built on 10,325 real shipment records ($68.8M in discrete freight spend). Data was sourced from the USAID Supply Chain Shipment Pricing Dataset (public domain, Kaggle), cleaned in Excel, and visualized in a 4-page Power BI dashboard covering spend by mode, country, vendor, and cost efficiency. Key findings: air and air charter freight represents 74% of total spend ($51M), Nigeria accounts for 22% of total spend ($15M) and ranks in the top 3 highest cost-per-kilogram countries — double exposure — and vendor rate outliers reach $103/kg on isolated shipments with no apparent rate agreement in place. Recommended actions include a mode-shift feasibility review targeting $4–5M in recoverable spend, a Nigeria lane RFQ, and a vendor rate compliance audit.
